# Project status (updated Nov 15)

**Status:** The website is built and deployed online for your review. All main
pages are working, and we are now filling in the content.

## What's done so far

- Built the website with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages
- Designed the site to look like a sari-sari store you look into through the rehas (grill window)
- **Tindahan (Home):** a crowded sari-sari storefront with a hand-painted signboard, awning, packed shelves behind a wire-mesh grill, and a pass-through window where each product is handed out before it opens one of our five stories (Tingi, Utang, Tambayan, Signs & Labels, Sachet Economy). Reworked Nov 15 after our professor's feedback; see `04-storefront-v2.md`
- **Paninda (Gallery):** all five stories plus a wall of six credited photos that open larger when clicked
- A page for each story, styled like a page from the tindera's listahan, each with full text, a photo and its sources
- **Tungkol sa Tindahan (About):** the project, why we chose the sari-sari store, and the team
- **Pabili! (Contact):** a contact form styled as an order slip that prints a receipt (prototype, sends nothing)
- Applied the elements of art and principles of design, including perspective on the shelves and awning, texture from plywood, paint and sachets, and rhythm in the hanging sachets
- Added animations and transitions: a roll-up shutter on the first visit, buying each product through the store's window (coin in, product out, story page out of the window), swaying hanging goods, and page-turn effects between stories
- Works on desktop and mobile (checked at 390px wide on Home, Paninda, a story page and Pabili!)
- Process documentation started: moodboard, wireframe, and captioned screenshots of every finished page (`docs/process/`)

## What's left

- Our own fieldwork: photographs of local sari-sari stores (the photos on the site are openly licensed stand-ins from Wikimedia Commons, credited on each page and on Tungkol), and real prices from a store for the Sachet story
- A photo of a real listahan page (names covered) for the Utang story; none with an open licence exists online
- Elijah and Aezekiel's read-through of the story wording; the Turgo (2013) source was checked from its abstract only
- Finishing the documentation PDF (moodboard v2 with our own photos, final screenshots)

*Any changes pushed to GitHub are reflected on the live site, so you can check
the link anytime to see our most recent progress.*
