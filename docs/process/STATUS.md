# Project status (updated Nov 9)

**Status:** The website is built and deployed online for your review. All main
pages are working, and we are now filling in the content.

## What's done so far

- Built the website with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages
- Designed the site to look like a sari-sari store you look into through the rehas (grill window)
- **Tindahan (Home):** a storefront with a hand-painted signboard, awning and shelves, where each product opens one of our five stories (Tingi, Utang, Tambayan, Signs & Labels, Sachet Economy)
- **Paninda (Gallery):** all five stories plus a wall reserved for our fieldwork photos (empty until fieldwork is done)
- A page for each story, styled like a page from the tindera's listahan
- **Tungkol sa Tindahan (About):** the project, why we chose the sari-sari store, and the team
- **Pabili! (Contact):** a contact form styled as an order slip that prints a receipt (prototype, sends nothing)
- Applied the elements of art and principles of design, including perspective on the shelves and awning, texture from plywood, paint and sachets, and rhythm in the hanging sachets
- Added animations and transitions: a roll-up shutter on the first visit, products moving from the shelf into their stories, and page-turn effects between stories
- Works on desktop and mobile (checked at 390px wide on Home, Paninda, a story page and Pabili!)
- Process documentation started: moodboard, wireframe, and captioned screenshots of every finished page (`docs/process/`)

## What's left

- Writing the full content for the five stories
- Fieldwork: photographing local sari-sari stores and adding the photos to the site
- Adding our sources and credits
- Final design refinements and testing
- Finishing the documentation PDF (moodboard v2 with our own photos, high-fidelity screens, final screenshots)

*Any changes pushed to GitHub are reflected on the live site, so you can check
the link anytime to see our most recent progress.*
