# 03 — Finished site: screenshots and captions

Captured Nov 9 from the local files at 1440×900 (Chrome headless).
Images are in `screenshots/`. Each caption names the element or principle used.

## Homepage — `screenshots/03-site-home.png`
The hand-painted **Tingi** sign is the **emphasis**: biggest, reddest, tilted,
with yellow trim. **Balance**: the sign is centered over symmetric shelves.
The striped awning and the dark rehas bars are **line**; the bars frame the
shelves and lead the eye in (**perspective**). Each product is a **form** in the
sachet palette, and the bunting repeats colors for **rhythm**. The shelves are
**space**: packed, but each product has room and a label. **Proportion**: big
sign, small products.

## Paninda (gallery) — `screenshots/04-site-paninda.png`
**Harmony**: the same red sign, teal wall and cream cards as the homepage.
Five equal cards are **rhythm** and **balance**. The red "Critique" stamp on
Sachet economy is **emphasis** by contrast. Teal wall lines are **texture**.

## Story pages — `screenshots/05-site-story-tingi.png`
(Utang, Tambayan, Signs & labels and Sachet economy use the same layout:
`06-…` to `09-…`.)
The notebook page uses ruled **lines** and a red margin to look like a
listahan. **Texture**: paper and tape. **Emphasis**: the yellow highlighter on
the key sentence. **Harmony**: Bungee for titles, Nunito Sans for body, Caveat for
handwritten questions. **Space**: a narrow centered column keeps the text readable.

## Tungkol sa Tindahan — `screenshots/10-site-tungkol.png`
Light card against a dark wooden panel: **contrast** and **balance** between two
blocks. The sign header repeats the **emphasis** pattern from every other page.

## Pabili! (contact) — `screenshots/11-site-pabili.png`
The form is styled as an order slip: dashed tear line (**line**), paper
**texture**, handwritten "Order slip" label. The yellow selected chip is the
**emphasis** for the current choice. Large inputs and generous **space** keep it
easy to fill in.

## 404 — `screenshots/12-site-404.png`
A hanging sign (**perspective**: it hangs in front of the wall) says "Ubos na!",
the shop's way of saying sold out. The shelf below reuses the product **forms**
to offer another way back: **harmony** even on the error page.
