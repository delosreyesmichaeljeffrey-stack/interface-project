## Update (from Tingi-Research-Sources.pdf)

**Resolved and now cited on the pages**
- Sachet figures: GAIA 2019 (Table 7: 163,732,476 sachets a day; GAIA 2020 summarizes it as 164 million a day, 59.8 billion a year). The page says "about 164 million a day, close to 60 billion a year".
- Sachets hard to recycle and costly for cities: GAIA 2020 (Liamzon et al.).
- Vernacular design definition: Meggs & Purvis (2012), p. 475; Philippine example from Balaguer, *Tropico Vernacular* (2016).
- Store credit notebooks, unpaid-debt losses and a paid debt left on the list by mistake: Silverio (1975/1982); unrecoverable credit: Custodio (2017).
