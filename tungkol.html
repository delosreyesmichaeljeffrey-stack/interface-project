Tungkol sa Tindahan

Ano ang Tingi?

Tingi is a website you enter like a sari-sari store. Every product on the shelf opens a story about Filipino community life.

Almost every Filipino neighborhood has a sari-sari store, and almost everyone has bought something from one. We see it as more than a store: it is where community identity, everyday economics and neighborhood stories meet.

So instead of a usual website, we built a storefront you look into through the rehas, with shelves you can browse.

Bakit sari-sari store?

Community identity
The store is a shared public space. It holds the stories, habits and humor of the people around it.
Everyday economics
Tingi and utang show how families get by one day at a time, built on trust between neighbors.
A critical look
We also ask who profits from the sachet economy, so we show both sides instead of romanticizing poverty.

Para saan ito?

Tingi is our final requirement for the Digital Interface Design Project. It applies the elements of art and principles of design to an interface that narrates community identity.
